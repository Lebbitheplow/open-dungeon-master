# Sources for the gameplay montage

All video is continuous footage recorded from Open Dungeon Master in dark mode, using an isolated copy of the demo campaign database. Combat was staged using the game's player actions and human DM rules engine; rolls and outcomes were actually resolved by that engine. Footage has fixed crops, cuts, captions, and limited speed changes. No screenshots are used as video sources.

The dice clacks use the game's bundled dice tray sound assets from `public/dice-box/sounds/surfaces/`. Other foley and editing effects are drawn from Kenney's CC0 packs. These are sound design added during editing, not a recording of the browser's audio output. There is no music in this version.

- [RPG Audio — Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio)
- [Interface Sounds — Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
- [Impact Sounds — Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)

Downloaded packs, including their original license files, are retained under `assets/v2/sfx/`. Fonts come from the game's bundled fonts.
